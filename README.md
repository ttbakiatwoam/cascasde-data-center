# Cascade Data Center

Research, source material, community questions, and policy analysis related to proposed data-center development in Cascade, Iowa.

## Purpose

This repository supports an evidence-based review of the proposed Cascade data-center project and the public discussion surrounding it. The goal is not to treat data centers as inherently good or bad, but to separate verifiable facts from speculation and identify enforceable protections that make sense for Cascade.

The working position of this project is **pro-data-center development with appropriate community protections, transparency, and accountability**.

## Dataset

The [`dataset/`](dataset/) directory converts the major research threads developed in this project into reusable Markdown notes with public source links.

Start here:

- [`dataset/00_INDEX.md`](dataset/00_INDEX.md) — dataset contents, methodology, privacy rules, and evidence labels
- [`dataset/21_OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_EVIDENCE_GAPS.md`](dataset/21_OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_EVIDENCE_GAPS.md) — living tracker of what is documented, represented, preliminary, unknown, or awaiting legal review
- [`dataset/22_SOURCE_INDEX.md`](dataset/22_SOURCE_INDEX.md) — consolidated public source and document index

The public dataset intentionally excludes Facebook comments/reply drafting, screenshots of private/local discussions, private correspondence, personal disputes, law-enforcement correspondence, addresses/contact information, and other personally identifying information.

## Areas of focus

This project tracks questions and research involving:

- Project ownership, intended use, scale, phases, and future expansion
- Electrical demand, utility infrastructure, grid impacts, and who pays for upgrades
- Cooling technology and water consumption
- Noise, tonal noise, low-frequency sound, and enforceable property-line limits
- Backup generation, emissions, fuel storage, and testing schedules
- Stormwater, wastewater, electronic waste, and other environmental impacts
- Construction and permanent employment
- Wages, local hiring, and workforce-development opportunities
- Property taxes, abatements, incentives, TIF, and clawback provisions
- Roads, traffic, fiber, water, sewer, and other infrastructure
- Fire protection, emergency response, batteries, UPS systems, and hazardous materials
- Lighting, security, setbacks, and neighboring-property impacts
- Independent environmental and acoustical studies
- Public reporting, inspections, complaint procedures, and ongoing compliance
- Decommissioning requirements and financial assurance
- Comparable operating facilities and documented real-world impacts
- Public claims and their underlying source evidence

## Community forum questions

A structured set of questions has been developed for Simple Mining and other project stakeholders. The questions are intended to obtain specific answers about the **Cascade project itself**, rather than relying on assumptions drawn from unrelated data centers, cryptocurrency facilities, AI facilities, or projects in other communities.

Current root data/tool files include:

- `questions.csv` — 76 community-forum questions organized by topic
- `submit-questions.sh` — optional local helper script for submitting the questions in `questions.csv`

The public copy of the submission helper contains no participant name or address. It prompts the operator to provide their own required identifying information at run time. The helper is designed to stop on rate limits, anti-abuse challenges, failed requests, or unexpected responses and is not intended to bypass CAPTCHA or other access controls.

## Policy principles under consideration

Potential local protections being evaluated include:

- Closed-loop or otherwise water-efficient cooling where technically appropriate
- Developer-funded independent environmental review
- Water-use reporting and enforceable limits where justified
- Measurable property-line and nighttime noise standards
- Independent post-construction acoustical testing
- Developer responsibility for project-driven infrastructure costs
- Emergency-services coordination and project-specific training/equipment support
- Reasonable lighting, setback, drainage, and traffic requirements
- Local workforce and training opportunities
- Clearly defined incentive limits and performance clawbacks
- Ongoing compliance reporting
- A funded decommissioning plan or other financial assurance

## Research standard

Where possible, claims should be traced back to primary or high-quality sources such as:

- City and county records
- Iowa statutes and administrative rules
- Utility filings and engineering documents
- Company statements and project documents
- Environmental and acoustical studies
- Peer-reviewed research
- Government agencies
- Original local reporting

Opinion pieces, advocacy material, podcasts, and national reporting may be useful for identifying claims, but they should not be treated as substitutes for the underlying evidence.

The dataset distinguishes public records, company representations, secondary reporting, and project-generated analysis rather than presenting them as equally authoritative.

## Project stance

A central principle of this repository is that Cascade should regulate the **physical and community impacts of the facility** that are within local government's lawful authority while avoiding unsupported assumptions about what computing workloads may eventually run inside a privately operated data center.

Questions about water, noise, electricity, taxes, infrastructure, emergency response, environmental impacts, jobs, and decommissioning deserve concrete answers and enforceable standards regardless of whether the machines inside are used for cryptocurrency mining, AI, cloud services, high-performance computing, or another lawful workload.
