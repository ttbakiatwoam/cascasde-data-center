# Open Questions and Evidence Gaps

> This file is a living evidence tracker. “Unknown” means the reviewed public record does not yet establish the answer; it does not imply that the information does not exist or that the applicant has refused to provide it.
>
> Last reviewed: 2026-09-18

## Status vocabulary

- **Documented** — supported by a public record or source document.
- **Represented** — stated by the company or in public discussion but not independently verified for the final design.
- **Preliminary** — reported for an early project concept and subject to change.
- **Unknown** — not established in the public sources reviewed.
- **Needs legal review** — depends on current local/state law or enforceability analysis.

## Comparable facilities

Still needed from the applicant:

- the operating Simple Mining site most technically similar to Cascade;
- site MW and equipment configuration;
- cooling/heat-rejection design;
- distance to nearby receptors;
- measured noise;
- actual water use;
- permanent staffing;
- documented complaints/violations, if any;
- local tax/infrastructure arrangement.

A named comparable site would allow Cascade to replace speculative analogies with a real operating dataset.
